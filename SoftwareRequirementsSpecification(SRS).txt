School Management System
Document Version: 1.0
Status: Product Master Specification
Technology Direction: Laravel / PHP
Database: MySQL or PostgreSQL
Primary Market: Private Schools in Pakistan
Initial Target Market: Middle-Tier Private Schools, District Attock, Punjab
Document Type: Software Requirements Specification
Prepared For: Product Development Team

1. Document Purpose
This Software Requirements Specification defines the complete functional and non-functional requirements for a cloud-based School Management and Learning Management Platform designed primarily for private schools in Pakistan.
The platform will provide a centralized digital environment through which school owners, management, administrators, teachers, accountants, students, parents, and support staff can manage and access school-related information and operations.
The system is intended to evolve into a multi-school platform capable of supporting hundreds of independent schools while allowing each school to maintain its own branding, configuration, academic structure, users, financial rules, grading system, reports, and operational workflows.
The initial implementation will be developed as a complete product architecture with a focused MVP containing the core modules required to operate a school digitally.

2. Product Vision
The product shall provide a single digital platform through which a private school can manage its complete day-to-day operation.
The platform should reduce dependence on:
● Paper registers
● Excel spreadsheets
● Manual fee records
● Separate attendance systems
● Manual result preparation
● Multiple disconnected applications
● WhatsApp-only communication
● Manual report generation
The long-term vision is:
One platform to manage the complete school - administration, academics, finance, communication, learning, staff, students and parents.

3. Product Objectives
The system shall aim to:
1. Digitize school administration.
2. Centralize student information.
3. Automate fee management.
4. Automate attendance management.
5. Simplify examination and result preparation.
6. Improve parent-school communication.
7. Give teachers a dedicated digital workspace.
8. Give parents access to their children's information.
9. Provide school owners with real-time management information.
10. Provide schools with an integrated learning platform.
11. Reduce administrative workload.
12. Improve data accuracy.
13. Provide actionable reports and analytics.
14. Support Pakistani school workflows.
15. Support multiple schools and campuses.
16. Allow school-specific configuration without modifying the core application.
17. Provide a foundation for future mobile applications and AI features.

62. Data Isolation
A school must never be able to access another school's data.
This requirement is critical.
Every school-specific query must enforce school ownership.
For example:
School A user:
SELECT only School A records.
School B user:
SELECT only School B records.
This must be enforced at the application architecture level rather than relying solely on frontend restrictions.

84. Product Design Principles
The system should follow these principles:
Simple
School staff should not need technical training.
Fast
Common actions should require minimal clicks.
Mobile-friendly
Teachers and parents will frequently use phones.
Pakistani
The system should reflect local school practices.
Configurable
Different schools must be able to configure their workflows.
Secure
Sensitive school and student information must be protected.
Scalable
The architecture must support hundreds of schools.
Modular
New modules should be addable without rewriting the core.

88. Final Development Principle
The most important architectural rule is:
Build the core system once, configure it for each school, and avoid creating a separate codebase for every school.
School-specific differences should primarily be handled through:
● Settings
● Permissions
● Configuration
● Templates
● Feature toggles
● Custom fields
● Workflow configuration
rather than modifying the core code for every customer.
This will allow the product to grow from:
1 school → 5 schools → 50 schools → 350 schools → potentially thousands of schools
without turning every new customer into a separate software-development project.
